# Decisions log: massing take-off and calculation template

Methodology decisions behind `takeoff/` and `calc/`. **Confirmed** means agreed by the office WLCA lead.
**Assumed** means a working default that still needs confirming. **Open** means not decided yet.
Drafted by an AI agent (Claude) from the working sessions; review before relying on it.

## Method and schema

| # | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | New interchange method value `Derived`: quantities from massing, schedule or plan rules, with a build-up applied. At RIBA 0–2 it is accepted where the default method is Benchmark, for elements flagged derivable in the scope matrix (1.1, 1.2.1–1.2.3, 2.1.1, 2.2.1, 2.5.1–2.5.3) | Confirmed |
| M2 | The area schedule is a valid GIA source; the header's `gia_source` names the file and sheet | Confirmed |
| M3 | Engineer's figures use the method `Supplied` and always override defaults | Confirmed |

## Floor arrangement

| # | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Two routes: (a) area schedule governs levels, FTF, GEA, GIA, slabs, roofs, including basements; (b) massing sliced at storey heights. The massing always governs façade | Confirmed |
| F2 | A volume counts as a storey only if a full storey height fits (`min_storey_fraction` = 1.0) | Assumed |
| F3 | GEA → GIA: inward offset of 0.30 m (façade zone). On the pilot tower this matched the schedule ratio, GIA/GEA = 0.963 | Confirmed |
| F4 | Massing vs schedule reconciled per level; differences above 3% are flagged | Assumed |

## Slabs, roofs, façade (BR-01 to BR-03)

| # | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Lowest level → 1.2.1. Suspended slabs below the ground line → 1.2.2. Ground floor and above → 2.2.1. The ground-floor slab over a basement counts as 2.2.1 because it is not *below* the ground line | Assumed (interpretation) |
| S2 | Any part of a storey not covered by the storey above is roof slab (2.2.1) plus roof coverings (2.5.3) | Confirmed |
| S3 | Basement roof outside the building footprint at ground level: slab 2.2.1 only; its finishes are external works 8.1.1 | Assumed (interpretation) |
| S4 | Overhang soffits are reported as insulated soffit (2.5.1), never as a second slab. v0.1 double-counted them; fixed in v0.2 | Confirmed |
| S5 | Façade split into 8 orientation bins centred on N, NE … NW. 4-bin boundaries made 45° façades ambiguous | Confirmed |
| S6 | Glazed area = storey façade × WWR. Opaque = envelope − glazed, so glazed + opaque equals the measured envelope | Confirmed |

## Substructure (no SI data)

| # | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| U1 | Foundations: bored piles, Ø0.75 m, 3 per column at the lowest level, 25 m long (user-adjustable) | Confirmed |
| U2 | Core pile group: core area ÷ (3 × Ø)² | Confirmed |
| U3 | Basement walls: perimeter = ground-floor footprint (massing), with an option for the engineer's figures | Confirmed |
| U4 | Hard/firm secant wall: Ø1.0 m piles, **1.2 m between hard-pile centres**, one firm pile centred between each pair. Overlaps are deducted once, from the firm piles. Per 1.2 m bay: 0.785 m² hard + 0.338 m² firm → equivalent to a 0.94 m wall | Confirmed |
| U5 | Secant wall length = basement depth × (1 + embedment ratio). Default ratio 0.5 (toe below formation level) | Assumed — confirm with engineer |
| U6 | RC liner, 200 mm, inside the piled wall = perimeter × basement depth | Confirmed |
| U7 | Firm piles use the C32/40 build-up as a placeholder; the real firm mix is lower strength | Open (needs a firm-mix factor) |
| U8 | Pile caps: one cap per column at the lowest level, sized for the piles per column (3 piles: equilateral triangle enclosing the piles at 3 × Ø, 0.15 m edge, corners not trimmed) × 1.2 m deep; plus a 2.0 m core cap over the core area + 0.5 m all round. The engineer's volume replaces both | Assumed |
| U9 | Basement larger than the ground footprint: scale the perimeter? (the script warns) | Open |

## Calculation template (`calc/`)

| # | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Workbook is generated by a script from three inputs (take-off export, build-up library, benchmark table) and keeps live formulas, so factors and inputs can be changed in Excel. No project or library data in this repo | Confirmed |
| C2 | Baseline A1–A3 factors: the library adopts the ETPP-28 (Arup 2025) baseline reference table where it overlaps, with the source kept per material (library v0.8) | Confirmed |
| C3 | Unitised curtain wall: build-up from the ETPP-28 unitised curtain wall bill of materials (per m² of façade incl. spandrel). CWCT method: factory assembly, transport to fabricator and fabrication waste in A3 (PS 5.1.4: offsite manufacturing waste belongs in A1–A3). CWCT site assembly is not added, because the A5.2 site lump covers it | Confirmed |
| C4 | Façade route switch on Project. "Unitised curtain wall" sends the opaque façade rows (the take-off's opaque build-up code, 2.5.1) to the curtain wall build-up and reports them in 2.5.2. Soffits are not routed | Confirmed |
| C5 | Benchmarks for elements not taken off: kgCO2e/m² GIA by use class (PS 4.5.2 method 2). The source covers shell & core + Cat A (building-related FF&E); Cat B loose items are excluded | Confirmed |
| C6 | Site lumps per m² GIA: A5.1 35 (existing GIA demolished), A5.2 40, C1 12. The PS Table 25 reading (25% of A5.2) would give C1 = 10 | Confirmed |
| C7 | Benchmark C1 shares are excluded by default, so C1 is not counted twice (element shares plus the whole-building lump). Switchable on Project | Assumed |
| C8 | A5.3 = waste rate × (A1–A3 + A3 offsite + A4 + C2–C4 of the wasted material). The rate is used directly, as in PS 5.1.4 and the office dwelling workbook. A layer can carry its own site rate | Assumed |
| C9 | B4 replacements = ROUNDUP(RSP / life) − 1 (PS 5.2.4: whole replacements, rounded up). **This differs from the dwelling workbook, which rounds down.** Curtain wall frames are 60 years; glass follows Table 20 (35 years), so it is replaced once | Assumed — differs from the dwelling workbook |
| C10 | End of life of replaced items goes in C2–C4 (× (1 + replacements)), as in the dwelling workbook | Assumed |
| C11 | A4 per material uses lorry loads: ROUNDUP(max(mass/payload, volume/capacity)) × Table 17 road km × (c1 + c2). DEFRA factors include the return leg, so the empty running factor is 1.0. The sea leg is per tonne-km, because ships are not loaded like lorries (the dwelling workbook's per-load sea factor does not scale) | Assumed |
| C12 | C2 uses loads to processing, EfW and landfill × distance × 2 × the 50%-laden factor (dwelling workbook). The Table 26 distances are placeholders (50 km) until the site is known | Assumed — set per site |
| C13 | C3/C4 default: Table 23 route × Table 28 landfill factor (PS 5.6.4: use landfill emissions when there is no processing data). The library can override per material (e.g. CWCT incineration for EPDM gaskets) | Assumed |
| C14 | D is a memo only: per-kg values for metals where the library has them, 0 elsewhere. Concrete D values in the source were not adopted | Assumed |
| C15 | Contingency: PS Table 10 percentage (15% at RIBA 0–2) on A–C as a separate row; not on D or biogenic carbon | Assumed |
| C16 | Modelled layers whose element group is benchmarked are dropped, so there is one method per element (BR-14) | Confirmed |
| C17 | Scope coverage in QA: any expected element with neither a quantity nor a benchmark is a GAP flag (PS 4.4.1). The pilot gaps are now covered by the allowances below (G1–G7) | Confirmed |
| C18 | Rows that rest on an office assumption carry `[ASSUMED]` at the start of their notes. The workbook marks them (Takeoff column Z), counts them in QA and shows their share of A1–A5 in the Results headline | Confirmed |

|---|---|---|

| V2 | The user picks which targets to show: UKNZCBS (limit for the year construction starts, UKNZCBS 4.2.5.3), LETI (upfront and life-cycle bands), RIBA 2030 Climate Challenge, GLA London Plan Guidance | Confirmed |
| V3 | Each target is compared on its own boundary: UKNZCBS (see V6) and LETI upfront on A1–A5 excl. external works; LETI life cycle and RIBA on A–C excl. external works; GLA on A1–A5 and A–C (excl. B6/B7) with all elements. Project values include contingency | Assumed |
| V4 | The headline always uses typical benchmarks for elements not yet designed. Target-aligned benchmarks are used only in a scenario (non-modelled elements scaled by GLA aspirational / benchmark) and in a carbon budget chart (target − modelled − site = what is left for the rest). Neither is a compliance result | Confirmed |
| V5 | LETI band = the first band whose limit the project does not exceed; a value just over a limit falls into the next band (no rounding) | Assumed |
| V6 | UKNZCBS offices have two upfront limits (Annex A). The shell & core limit applies to the shell & core assessment (5.1.6.2): our result less the Cat A share of element groups 3, 4 and 5, set on Project and 100% shell & core by default (strict: Cat A is never assumed away). The whole-building limit covers Cat B and loose FF&E (5.1.2.4), so it reads "not assessed" until a Cat B + loose FF&E allowance per m² is entered. Cat A-only and Cat B-only results are reported, not compared (5.1.2.4 NOTE 3). For offices the UKNZCBS headline and budget use the shell & core limit | Confirmed |
| V7 | GLA element comparison: GLA share of the typical development × GLA total for the use class, against the project's element group incl. contingency. Site A5.1/A5.2 sit inside the GLA groups, so they are not shown as a row | Assumed |
| V8 | Structure is compared with the Price & Myers ranges as a sense-check, not a target. P&M covers the structural engineer's scope only (foundations, basement walls, slabs, frame, roof slabs, structural stairs; no façade, finishes, secondary elements, services or external works) and includes site activities (A5a), so the project figure is modelled structure plus the A5.2 site lump (and C1 for A1–C4), before contingency. New-build groups by default; A1–A5 and A1–C4 ranges shown with a scope note | Confirmed |
| V9 | Branding (font, colours, logo) comes from a private config; without it the sheets use neutral defaults. Colour pairs were checked for colour-vision deficiency; every chart has data labels or a table beside it | Confirmed |
| V10 | Targets and limits are bare thresholds and carry no contingency. The dashboard shows the project before contingency and incl. the PS Table 10 contingency side by side, and draws the contingency as its own segment. The result is judged on the figure incl. contingency (the reportable figure, PS 4.10; UKNZCBS 5.1.4 requires the RICS uncertainty factor and recommends a design contingency, 4.2.5 NOTE 2). "Meets before contingency only" flags where the contingency alone tips a target over | Confirmed |

## Scope-gap allowances (take-off v0.8)

All of these run on office assumptions until project information replaces them: drawings, model quantities or figures supplied by the engineer or the user. Each rule takes the best source available, in this order: supplied (`source: "supplied"`), then drawing or model, then assumption. Rows that still rest on an assumption are tagged `[ASSUMED]`.

| # | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Stairs (2.4): count, flights, treads, width and going come from the plan drawing. The plan extractor v0.2 reads treads as thin rectangles; runs of 4 or more make a flight, and flights within 1 m make a stair. The stairs are in-situ RC, with the waist and landings assumed at 0.20 m. The volume per storey scales with storey height. Every storey except the top one is climbed | Assumed (geometry from drawing) |
| G2 | Edge beams (2.1.2): a 400 × 350 downstand on every slab edge from level 1 up, plus the top roof edge (the larger of the storey's perimeter and the perimeter of the storey below). Transfer structures are not assumed | Assumed |
| G3 | Louvres (2.6): 50% of the façade on plant levels above ground (from the schedule's Use column), taken out of the opaque façade | Assumed |
| G4 | Entrance doors (2.6): 2 entrances × 15 m², taken out of the glazed façade | Assumed |
| G5 | Other roof elements (2.3): an allowance per m² of roof covering plant supports, hatches and outlets | Assumed |
| G6 | Inverted roof (2.5.3) and insulated soffit (2.5.1): placeholders adapted from the residential library types until there are practice details | Assumed |
| G7 | Not yet allowed for: guarding and balustrades, lift pits | Open |
